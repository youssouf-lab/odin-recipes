Spaghetti

Description

Spaghetti and meatballs is a classic Italian-American comfort dish consisting of long, tender pasta topped with savory meatballs and a rich tomato sauce. It is famous worldwide for its hearty flavors and is a beloved staple for family dinners..

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti pasta
  • Ground meat
  • Breadcrumbs and egg
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion and garlic
  • Grated cheese and herbs

Steps

  1. Mix and shape the meatballs by combining the ground meat, breadcrumbs, egg, herbs, and seasoning, then rolling them into small balls
  2. Brown the meatballs in a large pan with a little oil until they are golden brown on all sides.
  4. Simmer in tomato sauce by adding the sauce, garlic, and onions to the pan with the meatballs and letting it cook until the meat is fully done
  5. Boil the spaghetti in a large pot of salted water according to the package instructions until it is al dente
  6. Combine and serve by tossing the drained pasta with the rich sauce and meatballs, then topping with grated cheese.
