Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a traditional baked pasta dish originating from Italy, made of wide, flat sheets layered with filling. It is one of the oldest and most famous Italian recipes in the world.

Ingredients

  • Pasta sheets
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Grated cheese

Steps

  1. Brown the ground beef in a pan until completely cooked
  2. Add the tomatoes to the meat and let it simmer into a simple meat sauce
  3. Layer the ingredients in a baking dish, alternating between pasta sheets, meat sauce, and grated cheese
  4. Top with cheese by adding a generous layer of grated cheese on the very top.
  5. Bake in the oven until the pasta sheets are soft and the top layer of cheese is melted and golden
