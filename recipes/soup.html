Soup

Description

Tomato soup is a classic, comforting dish made from blended ripe tomatoes, broth, and savory seasonings. It is loved worldwide for its smooth texture and rich, warming flavor, making it a perfect meal on its own or paired with grilled cheese.

Ingredients

  • Tomatoes
  • Onion and garlic
  • Olive oil or butter
  • Broth
  • Herbs

Steps

  1. Sauté the aromatics by heating butter or oil in a pot and cooking the chopped onion and garlic until sof
  2. Add the tomatoes and broth to the pot, bring the mixture to a boil, and let it simmer for about fifteen minutes.
  3. Blend the soup using an immersion blender until it reaches a smooth and consistent texture
  4. Season and finish by stirring in salt, pepper, herbs, and a splash of cream if desired, then serve hot
